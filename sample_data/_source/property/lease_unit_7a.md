## 3. Condition Report

Clause 3.1 The Landlord's agent will give the Tenant two copies of the ingoing condition report on or before the start date. The Tenant must return one signed copy within 7 days of moving in and may note any part of the report they disagree with.

Clause 3.2 The ingoing condition report is the main evidence of the state of the Premises at the start of the tenancy. It will be compared with the outgoing condition report when the tenancy ends and the bond is refunded.

## 4. Rent

Clause 4.1 Amount. The rent is $3,100 per month, payable in advance.

Clause 4.2 Due date. Rent is due on the 15th day of each month. The first payment of $3,100 is due on 1 November 2025, the start date of the tenancy, and covers the period to 14 December 2025 on a pro-rata basis.

Clause 4.3 Late payment. If rent remains unpaid 3 days after the due date, a late payment fee of $90 is charged for each late instalment. Repeated late payment may also be treated as a breach of this agreement under Clause 7.5.

Clause 4.4 Method of payment. Rent must be paid by bank transfer to the account shown in the rent schedule or through the Harbourview tenant portal. Cash is not accepted at the Harbourview office.

Clause 4.5 Dishonoured payments. If a rent payment is dishonoured or reversed by the Tenant's bank, a dishonoured payment fee of $25 is payable in addition to the rent.

Clause 4.6 Rent review. The rent may be reviewed no more than once in any 12 month period. The Landlord's agent must give the Tenant at least 90 days written notice of any rent increase, stating the new amount and the date it starts.

## 5. Bond

Clause 5.1 The Tenant must pay a bond of $6,200, equal to two months' rent, before the keys are handed over.

Clause 5.2 The Landlord's agent will lodge the bond with the Residential Bond Authority within 10 business days of receiving it and give the Tenant the bond lodgement number.

Clause 5.3 At the end of the tenancy the bond will be refunded, less any amounts owed for unpaid rent, damage beyond fair wear and tear, or cleaning needed to return the Premises to the condition shown in the ingoing condition report.

## 6. Utilities and Outgoings

Clause 6.1 The Tenant pays for electricity, internet and water usage charges. Building C is all-electric and has no gas supply. Water usage is billed quarterly by the Landlord's agent at cost.

Clause 6.2 The Landlord pays council rates, strata levies and fixed water service charges.

## 7. Term and Termination

Clause 7.1 Fixed term. The tenancy is for a fixed term of 12 months, starting on 1 November 2025 and ending on 31 October 2026.

Clause 7.2 Notice by the Tenant. To end the tenancy at the end of the fixed term, or at any time during a periodic tenancy after the fixed term, the Tenant must give 30 days written notice to the Landlord's agent. Notice may be given by letter or by email to the property manager, and the notice period starts on the day the notice is received.

Clause 7.3 Early termination by the Tenant. If the Tenant ends the tenancy before the fixed term expires, the Tenant must pay an early termination fee equal to two months' rent ($6,200), plus reasonable advertising and re-letting costs of up to $800. Rent remains payable until the Tenant hands back all keys.

Clause 7.4 Notice by the Landlord. The Landlord may end the tenancy at the end of the fixed term by giving at least 30 days written notice before the end date, or may end a periodic tenancy by giving at least 90 days written notice.

Clause 7.5 Breach. If either party breaches this agreement, the other party may give a 14 day notice to remedy the breach. If the breach is not remedied within 14 days, the tenancy may be terminated in accordance with the residential tenancy laws.

Clause 7.6 Continuation. If neither party gives notice, the tenancy continues as a periodic month-to-month tenancy on the same terms after 31 October 2026.

## 8. Vacating the Premises

Clause 8.1 When the tenancy ends, the Tenant must leave the Premises clean and in the same condition as at the start of the tenancy, apart from fair wear and tear.

Clause 8.2 The Tenant must return all keys, both building fobs and both garage remotes to the Harbourview office by 5:00 pm on the last day of the tenancy. Rent continues to be charged until all keys are returned.

Clause 8.3 The Landlord's agent will carry out the final inspection within 5 business days of the Tenant vacating and will prepare the outgoing condition report.

## 9. Maintenance and Repairs

Clause 9.1 The Tenant must report any damage or need for repairs to the Landlord's agent as soon as possible, and in any case within 3 days of becoming aware of it. Urgent repairs are handled under the Harbourview Maintenance and Repairs Policy.

Clause 9.2 The Tenant is responsible for replacing light globes, keeping sinks and drains clear, including the balcony floor drain, and cleaning the air conditioning return-air filter every three months.

Clause 9.3 The Landlord is responsible for keeping the Premises in reasonable repair, including plumbing, electrical wiring, appliances supplied with the Premises and smoke alarms.

Clause 9.4 Access. Except in an emergency, the Landlord's agent must give the Tenant at least 24 hours notice before entering to carry out repairs, and at least 7 days written notice before a routine inspection. Routine inspections are carried out no more than 4 times in any 12 month period.

## 10. Pets

Clause 10.1 No cats or dogs may be kept at the Premises. In line with the Building C House Rules, the Tenant may keep small caged birds, or fish in an aquarium of up to 50 litres.

Clause 10.2 This clause does not prevent the Tenant from keeping an assistance animal, provided the Tenant gives the building manager a copy of the assistance animal certification before the animal moves in.

## 11. Smoking

Clause 11.1 Smoking, including e-cigarettes and vaping, is not permitted anywhere in the Premises, including on the balcony.

## 12. Special Conditions
