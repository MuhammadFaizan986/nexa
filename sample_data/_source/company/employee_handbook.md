# Lumen Labs Employee Handbook

Synthetic demo document - fictional company, not real data.

Version 4.2, effective 1 January 2026. This handbook applies to all permanent full-time and part-time employees of Lumen Labs Pty Ltd. Part-time employees receive the entitlements below on a pro-rata basis. If this handbook differs from your employment contract, your contract applies.

## Welcome

Lumen Labs builds the Lumen Platform, a data platform used by more than 400 companies. We are a team of about 180 people across our Port Ellison headquarters and remote locations. This handbook explains how we work together and what you can expect from us.

## Working Hours

The standard working week is 38 hours. We use flexible hours: you may start between 7:00 am and 10:00 am and finish between 3:00 pm and 6:30 pm, as long as you are available during our core hours of 10:00 am to 3:00 pm, Monday to Friday.

Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager. Instead of overtime pay, employees can take time off in lieu at the rate of one hour off for each hour worked, to be used within 3 months.

## Where to Find Help

Questions about pay, leave or benefits go to the People team at people@lumenlabs.example. Leave requests, payslips and personal details are managed in PeopleHub. Questions about laptops, accounts and security go to the IT Service Desk at it@lumenlabs.example.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year during their first two years of service. From the third year of continuous service, the entitlement increases by one day for each completed year of service, up to a maximum of 25 days per year:

- Up to 2 completed years of service: 20 days per year
- After 3 completed years of service: 21 days per year
- After 4 completed years of service: 22 days per year
- After 5 completed years of service: 23 days per year
- After 6 completed years of service: 24 days per year
- After 7 or more completed years of service: 25 days per year

Annual leave accrues progressively each pay period. Requests must be made in PeopleHub at least 2 weeks in advance for leave of up to 5 days, and at least 4 weeks in advance for longer periods.

Up to 10 days of unused annual leave can be carried over into the next calendar year. Any balance above 10 days on 31 December must be taken by 31 March of the following year, or your manager may direct you to take it.

## Public Holidays

Employees are entitled to all public holidays in the state where they normally work. If you work on a public holiday at your manager's request, you receive a replacement day off to be taken within 3 months.

## Company Shutdown

Lumen Labs closes between Christmas Day and New Year's Day. The three working days of the shutdown are paid and do not come out of your annual leave balance.

## Sick and Carer's Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick and carer's leave per year. Unused sick leave carries over from year to year with no maximum. You can use it when you are unwell, or to care for a member of your household or immediate family who is sick or injured.

Tell your manager as early as possible on the first day of absence. A medical certificate or statutory declaration is required for absences of more than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

Employees with at least 12 months of continuous service are entitled to paid parental leave when they become a parent through birth, adoption or permanent foster care.

- Primary carer: 16 weeks of paid parental leave at full pay.
- Secondary carer: 4 weeks of paid parental leave at full pay, to be taken within 12 months of the child's arrival.
- Superannuation continues to be paid during paid parental leave.

Employees may take up to 12 months of parental leave in total, including the paid period, and may request a further 12 months of unpaid leave. Please give the People team at least 10 weeks notice before your expected start date.

## Other Leave

Employees can take 2 days of paid compassionate leave each time a member of their immediate family or household dies or suffers a life-threatening illness, and up to 3 days of paid community service leave per year for volunteer emergency work.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Lumen Labs is a hybrid company. Most employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. Our office anchor days are Tuesday and Wednesday, when teams are expected to be in the office together unless they are fully remote under their contract.

To support remote work, each employee receives a one-off home office allowance of $500 in their first month and an internet allowance of $40 per month, paid through payroll. Equipment bought with the home office allowance belongs to the employee.

You must follow the IT Security Policy when working outside the office, including using the company VPN on public Wi-Fi and never leaving your laptop unattended in public places.

## Working From Overseas

Employees may work from another country for up to 4 weeks in any 12 month period, with the written approval of their manager and the People team. Requests must be made at least 4 weeks before travel so that tax and security checks can be completed. Some countries are excluded for data protection reasons; the People team keeps the current list.

## Flexible Work Requests

If you need a different working pattern, such as compressed hours or reduced days, you can make a flexible work request to your manager. Your manager must respond in writing within 21 days.

## Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. During probation, either party can end the employment by giving 1 week of notice. Your manager will meet you for check-ins at 30 days, 90 days and before the end of probation.

## Notice Periods

After probation, the notice period to end employment depends on length of service:

- Less than 3 years of service: 4 weeks of notice
- 3 to 5 years of service: 6 weeks of notice
- More than 5 years of service: 8 weeks of notice

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Salary reviews are held once a year in July, based on the June review. Employees can ask for a development conversation with their manager at any time.

## Learning Budget

Every employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per financial year for courses, conferences, books and certifications related to their role. Up to 3 days of paid study leave per year can be taken for exams and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over.

## Expenses

Work expenses must be submitted in Expensely within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred. Expenses submitted later than 30 days may not be reimbursed.

- Receipts are required for every expense above $50.
- Expenses above $500 need your manager's approval before you spend the money.
- Meals while travelling for work: up to $80 per day.
- Flights: economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy for longer flights.
- Accommodation: standard business hotel, up to $250 per night in capital cities.
- Personal items, alcohol outside approved team events, and traffic or parking fines are not reimbursed.

Reimbursements are paid with the next pay run after approval. Company credit cards are available for employees who travel regularly; ask the Finance team.

## Company Equipment

Each employee receives a laptop, which remains company property and must be returned on the last day of employment. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk as described in the IT Security Policy.

## Code of Conduct

We expect everyone at Lumen Labs to act with honesty, respect and care for our customers and each other. Bullying, harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

Gifts or hospitality from customers or suppliers with a value above $100 must be declared to the People team within 5 business days. Gifts of cash are never accepted, whatever the amount.

Employees must declare any conflict of interest, such as a second job, a shareholding in a competitor or a family relationship with a supplier, to their manager and the People team. Declarations are reviewed every year.

## Raising Concerns

If you see something that does not feel right, speak to your manager or the People team. You can also report concerns confidentially to ethics@lumenlabs.example or through the anonymous whistleblower hotline on 1800 555 390. Lumen Labs does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
