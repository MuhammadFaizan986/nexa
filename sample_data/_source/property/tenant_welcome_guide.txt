Welcome to your new home at Harbourview Residences. This guide explains what to do in your first weeks as a tenant. It does not replace your residential tenancy agreement; if anything in this guide differs from your lease, the lease applies.


1. COLLECTING YOUR KEYS

Keys are collected from the Harbourview office at Level 2, 8 Ferry Lane from 10:00 am on the start date of your lease, once your bond and first rent payment have cleared. You will receive two apartment door keys, two building fobs, one mailbox key and one garage remote for each allocated car space.

Lost items can be replaced at the office. A replacement building fob costs $65 and a replacement garage remote costs $120. Lost fobs are deactivated on the same day you report them.


2. BOND LODGEMENT

Your bond is lodged with the Residential Bond Authority within 10 business days of payment. The Residential Bond Authority will email you a receipt with your bond number. Keep this email: you will need the bond number to claim your refund at the end of your tenancy using a joint claim form signed by you and Harbourview.


3. INGOING CONDITION REPORT

You will receive two copies of the ingoing condition report. Check every room carefully, add your comments and take dated photos of any existing damage. Return one signed copy to Harbourview within 7 days of moving in. The report protects your bond, so do not skip this step.


4. CONNECTING UTILITIES

Electricity: you choose your own electricity retailer. Arrange connection at least 3 business days before you move in so the power is on when you arrive.

Gas: Buildings A and B have gas hot water and cooking. Building C is all-electric and has no gas supply.

Water: water usage is billed quarterly by Harbourview at cost, based on your apartment's meter reading.

Internet: every apartment is connected to the fibre network. You choose and arrange your own internet provider.


5. PAYING RENT

Pay your rent by bank transfer or through the tenant portal at portal.harbourview.example. We recommend setting up an automatic payment a few days before the due date shown in your lease. Rent receipts and your rent ledger are available in the portal at any time.


6. MAIL AND PARCELS

Each lobby has smart parcel lockers. When a parcel arrives you will receive a locker code by SMS. Parcels not collected within 5 days are returned to the sender.


7. RUBBISH AND RECYCLING

Waste chutes, recycling bins and bulky waste collection rules differ slightly between buildings. Please read the house rules for your building, available in the tenant portal and from your building manager.


8. CONTENTS INSURANCE

The landlord's building insurance does not cover your furniture, clothes, electronics or other belongings. We strongly recommend taking out contents insurance from your first day.
