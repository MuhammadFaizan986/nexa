# Kestrel Pay Customer FAQ

*Synthetic demo document — fictional company, not real data.*

Answers to the questions our customers ask most often. Last updated 1 August 2026.

## How do I dispute a transaction?

If you see a card transaction you do not recognise, or you paid for something that never arrived, open the Kestrel Pay app, go to Transactions, select the transaction and tap Report an issue. You must lodge a dispute within 60 days of the date of the statement on which the transaction first appeared.

For disputes over $50, we give you a provisional credit for the disputed amount within 10 business days while we investigate. Most disputes are resolved within 45 days. If the dispute is decided against you, the provisional credit is reversed and we give you 5 business days notice first. There is no fee to lodge a dispute.

## What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?

Freeze your card straight away in the app under Cards > Freeze card. Freezing is instant and you can unfreeze the card if you find it. If you think the card has been stolen, call us 24 hours a day, 7 days a week on 1300 555 247 so we can block it permanently.

A replacement card costs $10 and arrives in 5 to 7 business days. Express delivery costs $25 and arrives in 2 business days. You can add your new card to your mobile wallet as soon as it is issued, before the physical card arrives.

## What are my transfer limits?

The default daily limit for outgoing transfers is $20,000 for Everyday and Kestrel Plus accounts and $100,000 for Business accounts. Personal customers can raise their limit to a maximum of $50,000 per day in Settings > Limits after completing an extra identity check.

For your security, payments to a new payee are limited to $5,000 in total during the first 24 hours after the payee is added.

## How long do transfers take?

Payments to other Australian bank accounts using Fast Payments usually arrive within 60 seconds, 24 hours a day. If the receiving bank does not support Fast Payments, the payment arrives by the next business day. International transfers usually arrive within 1 to 3 business days. International transfers confirmed after 4:00 pm (Sydney time) are processed on the next business day.

## How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap Forgot password and enter the email address on your account. We will send a 6-digit verification code by SMS, which is valid for 10 minutes. After 5 failed login attempts, your account is locked for 30 minutes to protect it.

## How do I close my account?

Transfer your remaining balance to another account, then request closure in the app under Settings > Close account. There is no fee to close an account. Closing an account also cancels its cards. Any Kestrel Flex credit balance must be repaid before the account can be closed.

## How do I contact Kestrel Pay?

Use the in-app chat, available 24 hours a day, or call 1300 555 247. Our phone team is available from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm (Sydney time) every day, and the lost and stolen card line is open 24 hours.
