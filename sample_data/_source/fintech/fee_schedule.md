# Kestrel Pay Fee Schedule

Synthetic demo document - fictional company, not real data.

This fee schedule applies to all Kestrel Pay personal and business accounts from 1 July 2026. It replaces the fee schedule dated 1 January 2026. All amounts are in Australian dollars and include GST where applicable.

## Account Fees

Kestrel Pay offers three account types. The monthly account fee is charged on the last business day of each month.

- Everyday account: monthly account fee $0
- Kestrel Plus account: monthly account fee $9, waived in any month in which at least $2,000 is deposited
- Business account: monthly account fee $15
- Paper statements: $2.50 per statement (electronic statements are free)
- Account closure: no fee

## Card Fees

- Replacement debit card: $10 per card. The first replacement in any 12 month period is free for Kestrel Plus customers.
- Express card delivery (2 business days): $25
- Domestic ATM withdrawals: no Kestrel Pay fee
- Overseas ATM withdrawals: $3.50 per withdrawal, plus any fee charged by the ATM operator
- Card transactions in Australian dollars: no fee

## Domestic Payments

Transfers between Australian bank accounts, including Fast Payments, are free on all account types. A fee of $30 applies to a request to trace or recall a domestic payment that was sent to the wrong account.

## International Transfers

Outgoing international transfers are charged a fee of 1.5% of the amount sent, with a minimum fee of $5 and a maximum fee of $50 per transfer. The fee is shown before you confirm the transfer.

Incoming international transfers are free. However, intermediary or overseas banks may deduct their own fees before the money reaches your account, and Kestrel Pay cannot refund these.

A request to trace, amend or recall an international transfer costs $30 per request.

## Foreign Exchange

When you send money overseas, spend in a foreign currency or withdraw cash from an overseas ATM, we convert the amount using the Kestrel reference rate plus a foreign exchange (FX) margin. The FX margin depends on your account type.

- Everyday account: FX margin of 0.9% above the Kestrel reference rate
- Kestrel Plus account: FX margin of 0.5% above the Kestrel reference rate
- Business account: FX margin of 0.6% above the Kestrel reference rate
- Weekend surcharge: an extra 0.25% applies to conversions made between 5:00 pm Friday and 9:00 am Monday (Sydney time), when currency markets are closed

FX quotes shown in the app are guaranteed for 30 seconds. If you confirm after the quote expires, the payment is rejected with error code E-410 and you will need to request a new quote.

## Kestrel Flex Credit Line Fees

Kestrel Flex is an optional credit line available to eligible Everyday and Kestrel Plus customers.

- Late payment fee: $25 if the minimum repayment is not received by the due date shown on the statement
- Dishonoured repayment fee: $15 if a scheduled repayment is rejected by your bank
- Cash advance fee: 3% of the amount withdrawn, with a minimum of $5
- Annual fee: $0
- Interest rate on purchases: 17.99% per year, with up to 45 interest-free days on purchases when the closing balance is paid in full

## Disputes

There is no fee to dispute a card transaction you do not recognise or a purchase that was not delivered as described. See the Customer FAQ for how to lodge a dispute and how long it takes.

## Changes to Fees

Kestrel Pay will give you at least 30 days notice of any new fee or increase to an existing fee, by email and in the Kestrel Pay app. Decreases to fees can take effect immediately.
