# Building C House Rules

*Synthetic demo document — fictional company, not real data.*

Harbourview Residences, Building C, 42 Quay Street, Port Ellison. These house rules were adopted by the Building C owners corporation on 3 February 2025 and apply to all owners, tenants, occupants and visitors. Tenants must also follow the terms of their residential tenancy agreement.

## 1. Pets

Cats and dogs are not permitted in Building C, including in apartments, on balconies and in common areas. Residents may keep small caged birds, and fish in an aquarium of up to 50 litres.

Assistance animals are permitted. The resident must give the building manager a copy of the assistance animal certification before the animal moves in. Visitors may not bring cats or dogs into Building C unless the animal is an assistance animal.

If an animal is kept in breach of this rule, the building manager will issue a breach notice and the animal must be removed within 14 days.

## 2. Quiet Hours

Quiet hours are from 10:00 pm to 7:00 am from Sunday night to Thursday night, and from 11:00 pm to 8:00 am on Friday and Saturday nights. During quiet hours, music, televisions and conversations must not be audible from other apartments or common areas.

Renovation work and power tools may only be used between 8:00 am and 5:00 pm on weekdays and between 9:00 am and 1:00 pm on Saturdays. No renovation noise is allowed on Sundays or public holidays.

## 3. Parking

- Each apartment's car spaces are allocated in its lease or title. Residents must only park in their own allocated bays.
- Visitor bays V1 to V6 are on the ground level. Visitors may park for a maximum of 4 hours between 8:00 am and 10:00 pm. Overnight visitor parking is not permitted.
- Vehicles parked in breach of these rules receive a warning sticker and may be towed at the owner's expense after 24 hours.
- Electric vehicles may be charged at the shared charging bays C-EV1 and C-EV2. Charging costs $0.35 per kWh and is billed monthly by the building manager.
- Bicycles must be stored in the bicycle room on basement level B1 and must carry a registration tag from the building manager.

## 4. Waste and Recycling

General waste must be bagged and placed in the waste chute on each floor between 7:00 am and 10:00 pm. Recycling goes in the yellow-lid bins in the bin room on basement level B1; glass and containers must be rinsed and cardboard flattened.

Bins are collected on Tuesdays and Fridays. Bulky items such as furniture can be collected on the first Wednesday of each month; bookings must be made with the building manager and are limited to 2 items per apartment.

## 5. Balconies

- Only electric barbecues may be used on balconies. Charcoal and gas barbecues are not permitted.
- Washing must not be hung over the balustrade and must not be visible from the street.
- Nothing may be stored above the height of the balustrade.
- Pot plants must have drip trays so that water does not run onto the balconies below.
- Smoking and vaping are not permitted on balconies or anywhere in the common areas.

## 6. Moving In and Out

Moves must be booked with the building manager at least 48 hours in advance so that protective pads can be fitted to the goods lift. Moves are permitted from Monday to Saturday between 8:00 am and 5:00 pm. A refundable lift bond of $200 is payable when booking and is returned after the lift is checked for damage.

## 7. Common Areas

The lobby, corridors, recreation lawn and basement must be kept clear of personal items. Children under 12 must be supervised in the recreation lawn area. Common area doors must not be propped open.

## 8. Breaches of the House Rules

For a first breach, the building manager will issue a written breach notice. If the breach continues or is repeated, the owners corporation may issue a formal notice to comply and, for tenants, will also notify the landlord's managing agent. Serious or repeated breaches may be referred to the tribunal.
